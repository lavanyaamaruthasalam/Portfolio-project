# Revenue Concentration & Pareto Analysis (Power BI)

This Power BI project analyzes revenue concentration across brands and products to evaluate whether the business follows the traditional Pareto (80/20) principle or demonstrates a more diversified revenue distribution.

The dashboard focuses on answering key business questions such as:

- Do a small number of brands generate the majority of revenue?
- What percentage of total revenue comes from the top-performing brands?
- Does the business follow the classic 80/20 rule?
- Which products contribute most within the highest revenue-generating brand?
- Is revenue concentrated or distributed across multiple products?

---

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Brand Revenue Concentration Dashboard

![Brand Revenue Concentration](screenshots/BR concentration.png)

This view provides a high-level analysis of revenue distribution across all product brands, including:

- Total Transactions, Quantity, Revenue, Profit, and Profit Margin
- Pareto chart showing brand-wise revenue and cumulative contribution
- Top-performing brands ranked by revenue
- Detailed contribution table
- Key business insights

#### Key Insight

- Top 10 brands contribute approximately **26.47%** of total revenue.
- The highest revenue-generating brand (**Hermanos**) contributes only around **3.2%** of total revenue.
- Revenue is broadly distributed across brands rather than concentrated among a few dominant players.

---

### 2. Product Revenue Concentration Dashboard

![Product Revenue Concentration](screenshots/PR concentration.png)

This dashboard drills into the top-performing brand (**Hermanos**) to analyze product-level contribution patterns, including:

- Total Revenue and Profit Margin within the selected brand
- Product-wise Pareto chart and cumulative contribution
- Product revenue ranking
- Top product contribution table
- Interactive brand slicer

#### Key Insight

- Even within the highest revenue-generating brand, revenue is distributed across multiple products.
- No single product dominates overall brand performance.

## Project Outcome

This dashboard demonstrates that the business does not strongly follow the traditional 80/20 Pareto principle.

Instead, revenue is distributed across a broad portfolio of brands and products, reducing dependency on a small number of contributors and indicating a more stable, diversified revenue structure.

The analysis highlights the importance of validating common business assumptions using actual data rather than relying solely on generalized principles.

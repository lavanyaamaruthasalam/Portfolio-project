## Projects

### Retail Sales Analysis
Comprehensive sales performance analysis using 269K+ retail transactions to evaluate revenue, profitability, return impact, and customer behavior.

### Revenue Concentration & Pareto Analysis
Analyzed revenue distribution across brands and products to test whether the business follows the Pareto (80/20) principle. Found that the top 10 brands contributed only 26.47% of total revenue, revealing a diversified long-tail revenue structure.

### Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis
Built a complete SQl-Based customer analytics pipeline using RFM(Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to identify high-value customers, customers at risk of churn, and retention opportunities. Discovered that nearly 70% of total revenue was driven by New Customers and an unclassified Others segment, while 25.5% of the customer base was identified as At Risk, highlighting immediate opportunities for segmentation refinement, retention and win-back strategies.

### Retrun Impact & Profitability Analysis
Evaluated the financial impact of product returns on business performance using SQL and Power BI. Measrured return rates, revenue loss, cost loss, net profitability and margin stability across products, brands, stores, regions and time periods. The analysis revealed that returns had a relatively limited impact on overall profitability, while business cost structure remained the primary driver of profit performance. Profit margins remained stable across key business dimensions, demonstrating resilient financial performance despite return activity.

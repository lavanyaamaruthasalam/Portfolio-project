## Projects

### Retail Sales Analysis
Comprehensive sales performance analysis using 269K+ retail transactions to evaluate revenue, profitability, return impact, and customer behavior.

### Revenue Concentration & Pareto Analysis
Analyzed revenue distribution across brands and products to test whether the business follows the Pareto (80/20) principle. Found that the top 10 brands contributed only 26.47% of total revenue, revealing a diversified long-tail revenue structure.

### Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis
Built a complete SQl-Based customer analytics pipeline using RFM(Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to identify high-value customers, customers at risk of churn, and retention opporunities. Discovered that nearly 70% of total revenue was driven by New Customers and an unclassified Others segment, while 25.5% of the customer base was identified as At Risk, highlighting immediate opporunities for segmentation refinement, retention and win-back strategies.
